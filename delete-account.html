Delete your TailorHub account

App: TailorHub  |  Developer: Legacy Digital

You can delete your TailorHub account and all the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open TailorHub and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. You will be logged out on all your devices.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot log in, send an email to support.legacydigital@gmail.com with the subject Delete my TailorHub account. Write the email address you used to sign up to TailorHub. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming your request.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login).
  • Your profile: shop name, tailor name, phone number, country and settings.
  • All customers, phone numbers, measurements, style options, orders, payments, notes and invoices you saved.
  • The list of devices linked to your account.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. If you want a copy first, use Export Data in Settings before you delete.

What we keep

We do not keep your account data after deletion. Purchase records for a Premium subscription are held by Google Play under Google's own rules, not by us. A copy of your data may stay on a device you used, until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Want to delete only some data?

You can delete single customers, measurements and orders inside the app without deleting your account.
