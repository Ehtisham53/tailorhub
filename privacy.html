TailorHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026  |  Developer: Legacy Digital

TailorHub is an app for tailors to save customer measurements, orders and payments. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using TailorHub you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and password (used to sign in), and the country you choose at sign-up. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and we cannot see them.

Business information you enter. Your shop name, tailor name and phone number, and the records you create in the app: customer names and phone numbers, measurements, style options, orders (fabric, colour, quantity, delivery date), payments, notes and invoices. You decide what to enter.

Device information. To enforce the number of devices allowed on an account, we store a device identifier and a basic device label for each device you are logged in on.

Subscription information. If you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only the plan status and expiry date. We never see your card or payment details.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and use your measurements, orders and payments on your devices.
  • To create invoices and measurement slips (PDF) that you can share.
  • To limit the number of devices on an account according to your plan.
  • To provide Premium features and customer support.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on servers in Asia (Singapore region). Some data is also kept on your device so the app keeps working without internet. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase and Google Play), or when the law requires it. Your customers' details are yours: we do not share them with other TailorHub users.

Sharing from the app

When you share an invoice or a measurement slip by WhatsApp or another app, you choose the app and the person. That file then follows the privacy rules of the app you chose.

Customer data you enter

You are responsible for the customer information you save in TailorHub, and for having your customers' permission to keep their measurements and phone numbers.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays in your account until you delete it. You can delete individual customers, measurements and orders in the app. You can delete your whole account from Profile > Settings > Delete Account, which permanently removes your account and all its data. You can also export your data from Settings. If you need help, contact us at the email below.

Children

TailorHub is meant for tailors and shop owners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We use Firebase security rules so that each account can read only its own data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and do not share it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date on this page.
